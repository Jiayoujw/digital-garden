The Zettelkasten (German for "slip box") is a note-taking method developed by sociologist Niklas Luhmann.

## Key Principles

1. **Atomicity**: Each note captures exactly one idea
2. **Connectivity**: Notes are densely linked together
3. **Emergence**: Structure emerges from connections, not folders
4. **Dialogue**: Notes "talk" to each other through links

## Why It Works

By creating many small, interconnected notes instead of long hierarchical documents, ideas can surprise you. A thought from one domain suddenly connects to another — this is the essence of creative thinking.
