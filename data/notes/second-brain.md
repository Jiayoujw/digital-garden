A "Second Brain" is a personal knowledge management system that extends your biological memory and thinking capacity.

## The Concept

Popularized by Tiago Forte, the idea is that we need an external system to:
- **Capture** ideas before they escape
- **Organize** information for retrieval
- **Connect** disparate thoughts
- **Create** new insights from existing knowledge

## Building Blocks

The [[zettelkasten-method]] is one of the earliest and most influential second brain approaches. Combined with:
- [[Daily Notes]] for capture
- [[Knowledge Graph]] for visualization
- [[graph-theory]] for understanding structure

## Your Digital Garden

This very application is a second brain. Each note is a thought, each [[wikilinks|link]] is a synapse, and the graph represents the shape of your thinking. The more you write and connect, the more valuable it becomes.
