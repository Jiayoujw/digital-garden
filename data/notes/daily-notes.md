Daily notes are a core practice in personal knowledge management — a space for unfiltered thinking, logging, and capturing the raw materials that become refined notes.

## Purpose

- **Low-friction capture**: No need to organize or title — just write
- **Temporal context**: Ideas are anchored to a specific date
- **Incubation**: Raw daily thoughts can grow into permanent notes

## Workflow

1. Start each day with a [[Daily Notes|daily note]]
2. Write freely — ideas, tasks, reflections
3. When an idea matures, extract it into its own note
4. Link back with [[wikilinks]]

This practice works well with the [[zettelkasten-method]] and strengthens your [[second-brain]].
