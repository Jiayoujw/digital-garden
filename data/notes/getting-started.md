Welcome to your Digital Garden! This is a place to grow your thoughts, connect ideas, and build a personal knowledge base.

## How It Works

- **Create notes** using the sidebar "New Note" button
- **Link notes** using [[wikilinks]] syntax - just wrap a note title in double brackets
- **Visualize** your knowledge on the [[Knowledge Graph]]
- **Write daily** thoughts in [[Daily Notes]]
- **Discover clusters** of related ideas automatically

## Tips

- Use `[[note-title]]` to create links between notes
- Use tags to categorize your thoughts
- The more you link, the richer your graph becomes
