A knowledge graph represents information as a network of interconnected nodes (concepts) and edges (relationships).

## Key Properties

- **Nodes** represent entities, ideas, or notes
- **Edges** represent connections or references between nodes
- **Clusters** emerge when related ideas naturally group together

## In Your Digital Garden

Every [[wikilinks|wikilink]] you create becomes an edge in your [[Knowledge Graph]]. The graph grows organically as you add more notes and connections.

This approach is inspired by:
- [[graph-theory]] in mathematics
- [[zettelkasten-method]] for note-taking
- Semantic web technologies
